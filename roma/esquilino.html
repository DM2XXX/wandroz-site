Esquilino — Rome, Italy

Average day Higher caution advised night

In detail

Esquilino, around Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II next to Termini station, is Rome's most multicultural central district, known for its market and immigrant-run shops alongside historic apartment blocks. It is the clearest documented hotspot in Municipio I: the Rome prefect signed an official "zona rossa" (red zone) ordinance in January 2025, giving police powers to issue urban bans against people with records for drug dealing, mugging or robbery across roughly fifteen named streets and Piazza Vittorio itself, later extended toward San Lorenzo and the Togliatti corridor. Throughout 2026, repeated police blitzes reported arrests for drug dealing, counterfeit goods and pickpocketing, and in January 2026 residents held a public protest over degrado and insecurity. Daytime brings market crowds and some pickpocketing/counterfeit-goods activity; the more serious, recurring issues are concentrated after dark.
