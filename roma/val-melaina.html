Val Melaina — Rome, Italy

Average day Higher caution advised night

In detail

Val Melaina is a mid-20th-century public-housing district and one of the areas in Municipio III most consistently flagged in Rome press for both building decay and crime. Press has repeatedly reported on crumbling ATER blocks and residents saying anyone can commit a crime here, while police described one specific public-housing unit as a drug-dealing base in an area with a documented high crime rate. Periodic multi-agency raids here have suspended businesses and issued tens of thousands of euros in fines, and there have been repeated evictions of illegally occupied ATER apartments. These are genuine, recurring, documented concerns, worse after dark.
