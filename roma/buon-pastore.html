In detail

Buon Pastore is a small, low-density zone named after the Complesso del Buon Pastore, a large early-20th-century religious institute building near Via di Bravetta, tucked against the Riserva Naturale della Valle dei Casali and Villa Doria Pamphilj. Press research found no crime or safety-related news tied specifically to this area - searches mostly return the religious institute itself and generic parish content, not police or crime reporting. With no evidence of concern found, it defaults to green for both day and night.
