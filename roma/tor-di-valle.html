Tor di Valle — Rome, Italy

In detail

Tor di Valle is the site of Rome's former horse-racing track, closed for years and at the centre of a long-stalled stadium redevelopment project; today it is largely disused, semi-abandoned land bordering the Tiber. Documented press issues include a fatal past assault on a cyclist along the adjacent bike path, older reports of a deteriorated train station and illegal waste dumping, and two large vegetation/debris fires at the derelict site in August 2026. There is no evidence of an ongoing violent-crime pattern, but the area's abandoned, poorly-lit, little-trafficked character combined with this documented history of neglect and one serious past assault warrants caution, so both day and night are rated yellow.
