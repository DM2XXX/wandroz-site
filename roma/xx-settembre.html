XX Settembre — Rome, Italy

Relatively safer day Average night

In detail

The XX Settembre area, around Via XX Settembre and Castro Pretorio, is a largely institutional and residential district of ministries, the Ministero della Difesa, and the US Embassy annex, bordering Termini station to its south. It sits at the edge of the official Termini/Esquilino "zona rossa" enhanced-surveillance zone and has seen some spillover of the same predatory-crime and drug-dealing concerns documented there. The government/embassy character of the area keeps a visible security presence, and no pattern of serious or recurring violent crime specific to this zone was found beyond that.
