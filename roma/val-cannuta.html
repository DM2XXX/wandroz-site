Val Cannuta — Rome, Italy

In detail

Val Cannuta is a residential area around the San Raffaele university campus and Villa San Feliciano clinic. The most notable documented issue was a former residence on via Val Cannuta, used for years as an emergency housing centre that press described as poorly managed, with unauthorized occupations and unsafe conditions; the facility was finally closed and returned to its private owner after tenant protests over relocation. Aside from that now-resolved situation and an isolated clinic break-in, no ongoing pattern of street crime or degrado is documented for the area.
