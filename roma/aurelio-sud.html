In detail

Aurelio Sud is the southern part of the Aurelio district, a mostly residential and commercial area bordering the Vatican/San Pietro area near via Gregorio VII. In 2026 local press reported a knife attack inside a home in the Aurelio area, stemming from a dispute over a money debt, with the victim hospitalized; Carabinieri also carried out an extraordinary territorial control operation with anti-drug dogs across Aurelio-San Pietro aimed at diffuse criminality, including drugs and robbery. These are the kind of periodic anti-crime sweeps seen in many Rome districts rather than evidence of an unusually dangerous area, and daytime activity around the shops and Vatican-adjacent streets shows no specific safety concerns in recent coverage.
