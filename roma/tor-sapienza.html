Tor Sapienza — Rome, Italy

Average day Higher caution advised night

In detail

Tor Sapienza is a working-class eastern quartiere that became internationally known in November 2014, when residents attacked a migrant reception centre on Viale Giorgio Morandi, escalating into nights of clashes with riot police that left 14 officers injured. Tension has recurred since: press reported the fatal shooting of a 24-year-old man in the street in 2023, and a further violent ambush in the area in July 2026. This is one of the most consistently and seriously documented safety concerns in Municipio 5, warranting a red night rating; by day the area functions as an ordinary residential district but with underlying social tension.
