Trullo — Rome, Italy

Average day Higher caution advised night

In detail

Trullo is a mid-20th-century public housing district (case popolari) adjoining Corviale and Magliana, today a mixed working-class residential quarter. Local press has documented a stabbing, burned cars, and persistent urban decay in the area, a narcotics operation that turned up drug packets branded with a crime-drama reference, and press has explicitly described Trullo as an emerging hub for street-level dealing; large joint carabinieri operations with helicopters have targeted Trullo and neighboring Corviale together. This is one of the more consistently documented problem areas in Municipio XI, with recurring drug-related police operations and at least one violent incident reported, so night-time caution is well warranted even though it functions as an ordinary, if visibly disadvantaged, residential district by day.
