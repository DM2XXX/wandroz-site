Centro Storico — Rome, Italy

Relatively safer day Average night

In detail

Centro Storico is Rome's historic tourist core - Pantheon, Piazza Navona, Trevi Fountain and the dense lanes around them, packed with restaurants, shops and hotels. Italian press through 2026 documents frequent police operations here, but the pattern is consistently petty crime, not violence: pickpocket gangs and tourist-scam rings (including the classic "three-shell game" con near the Fori) broken up by carabinieri, plus arrests for counterfeit-goods vendors. Municipio I brought in extra private security (Italpol patrols) and the area got a 30km/h traffic-calming scheme in January 2026 after complaints about crowding. No reports of muggings, stabbings or organized violent crime were found. Risk to visitors is mainly theft in crowded piazze and on buses, somewhat higher after dark when bars and street sellers are busiest.
