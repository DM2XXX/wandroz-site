Trionfale — Rome, Italy

Relatively safer day Average night

In detail

Trionfale is a large, mostly residential middle-class zone stretching up to Monte Mario, close to the Vatican. Local press in 2025 documented repeated informal encampments on the wooded slopes of Monte Mario, dismantled by police multiple times (including after a July 2024 wildfire that damaged part of the hillside), and residents quoted expressing ongoing concern about degrado and public health in the area around these sites. Carabinieri also ran joint control operations across Trionfale/Monte Mario/Ottavia in 2025-2026. These are localized issues tied to specific encampment spots rather than a broad neighbourhood-wide crime problem, so daytime is calm but the wooded/hillside pockets warrant some caution after dark.
