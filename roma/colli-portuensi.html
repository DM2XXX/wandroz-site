In detail

Colli Portuensi is a quiet, mostly residential hillside area of villas and apartment blocks in Monteverde. Italian press research turned up little beyond a single April 2025 armed robbery of a pharmacy on the street (a man fled with about 1,200 euros); no pattern of recurring street crime, muggings, or degrado was found. Given the isolated nature of that incident, days read as calm and green, with a cautious yellow at night reflecting that one documented armed robbery rather than any wider trend.
