In detail

Santa Cornelia is a small, quiet residential pocket near via Flaminia/Cassia in the far north of the municipio. The most notable documented incident dates to March 2019, when press reported assaults, illegal dumping and thefts tied to a nearby informal settlement, with residents describing years of intimidation before a joint Carabinieri-Polizia blitz addressed it. No comparable incidents surfaced in recent (2024-2026) local press; more recent coverage of the area concerns road-safety infrastructure works rather than crime. Given the seriousness (if dated) of that one report, a cautious yellow at night is warranted; daytime is calm.
