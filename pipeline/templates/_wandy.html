{# Wandy — the Wandroz guide. A location pin with a face, because the pin is what this whole site is: one shape dropped on one neighbourhood. Drawn inline rather than shipped as an asset so it stays crisp at any size, inherits the palette, and costs no request. Two colours and no more: teal body, navy features. Two things were wrong in the first drawing, and both were about size rather than taste. The viewBox was 80 wide for a pin that spanned 10 to 70, so a fifth of every box she was given went to empty margin and she rendered smaller than her own CSS width promised. And the face was small inside the pin, which put the eyes below the size at which they read as eyes at all. The box is now cropped to what is actually drawn, and the face takes more of the head. She reads at 40px, which is where a mark has to work. Restraint is still the brief — no blush, no eye highlights, no float. The wink is the only moving part, and it is an action rather than an expression: both eyes are open for most of the loop and the right one shuts for a beat every six seconds. So the resting state — what a screenshot catches, and what a reader with prefers-reduced-motion keeps on screen — is Wandy looking straight ahead. #}
