{# Wandy — the Wandroz guide. A location pin with a face, because the pin is what this whole site is: one shape dropped on one neighbourhood. Drawn inline rather than shipped as an asset so it stays crisp at any size, inherits the palette, and costs no request. Palette is the site's own: --accent-bright to --accent. Restraint is the brief. The first version reached for the baby schema — big eyes, highlights, blush, a float — and it read as a mascot on a site that publishes police statistics. What is left is a mark: clean geometry, two dots for eyes, a thin mouth, one small sparkle. It should sit beside the wordmark without embarrassing it. The wink is the only moving part, and it is an action rather than an expression: both eyes are open for most of the loop and the right one shuts for a beat every six seconds. So the resting state — what a screenshot catches, and what a reader with prefers-reduced-motion keeps on screen — is Wandy looking straight ahead. #}
