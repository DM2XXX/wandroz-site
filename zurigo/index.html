Zurich neighbourhoods

Click a neighbourhood on the map to see its level, the reasoning, and a Booking.com link for that area.

Neighbourhood shapes are the City of Zurich's real official boundaries (the "Statistische Quartiere" dataset). Safety levels are a first manual pass — general knowledge, not a geolocated crime dataset — unlike London. Where available, each neighbourhood page below also shows one real, official data point: the Kantonspolizei Zürich's burglary rate for its wider city district (Kreis) — narrower and coarser than London's pipeline, but genuine and current.
